Theme

Every prop: millimetres, Y up, origin bottom centre on the floor, front facing +Z. Materials are shared across props (one cache). Moving props — person, AMR, forklift, pallet jack — expose userData hooks (legs / arms / leds / forks / top). Labels shows the tag overlay.
